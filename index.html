🔢 SAC Rate Shortcut (Bar)

Metric tanks are rated in Liters

Gas used = Bar used × Tank L

Example: 50 bar used × 12L tank = 600L gas

🧮 MOD Quick Calc (Bar)

MOD = (PO2 ÷ O2%) × 10 - 10

EAN32 @ 1.4 PO2: (1.4 ÷ 0.32) × 10 - 10 = 33.75m
Quick: 32% → ~34m MOD

⏱️ Gas Time @ Depth

Time = AvailableGas ÷ (SAC × ATA)

At 20m (3 ATA): 1000L ÷ (20 L/min × 3) = 16.6 min

📐 Memory Tricks

🎯 EAN32: MOD ~34m (1.4 PO2)
🎯 EAN36: MOD ~29m (1.4 PO2)
💨 Average SAC: 15-20 L/min surface
🏊 Swim speed: ~15m/min normal
